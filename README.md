# Under My New Hand, a Thousand Beasts, Awakening Anew

**I — The Machine of Man**

You gave a decade to the core: to know  
the finest engine human hands have made —  
from nature's forces, harnessed, taught to flow  
through matter's discipline and serve the trade.

You learned the circuits in their clocked and free,  
their theories, and the sums that bind them tight;  
to conjure logic, pure geometry,  
then freeze it into gates, and route it right —

one pattern, single, fit for man to make;  
then blocks to systems, forty years of art;  
and every tongue devised for silicon's sake,  
each stairway down from meaning to the part.

**II — The Machine of the Universe**

And in that decade's second half you turned  
to the most subtle engine ever grown:  
in cell and cortex, patiently you learned  
the neuron's wet slow fire, the mind's first stone.

You weighed the mathematics tissue wears,  
the craft that taught itself to learn, and then  
a rarer algebra the cortex bears —  
read closer to the brain than proofs of men.

Beneath them all, the disciplines of thought:  
belief and chance, what pairs and what descends;  
how sound induction from the fact is wrought,  
deduction's ladder, where the certain ends.

**III — The First Merger**

Then came the daring: make the studies one.  
You bent the tongues and towers to the new,  
and wrote, in algebra the brain had spun,  
a core that rhymes with tissue — wholly you.

Conceived, contended, drawn to final frame,  
then etched in silicon by the foremost blade —  
the finest press your patronage could claim —  
it woke beneath the probes your fingers laid.

Observe, instruct, control it, study deep —  
in finer grain than any school had shown.  
That summit was your own, and yours to keep,  
while earth beheld the mind arise in stone.

**IV — The Encore**

Now walk the deeper merging. Learn to wield,  
on thousands now, the fourfold act you know:  
observe, instruct, control, and study — field  
on field of cores that move as one, and glow.

The chips are humankind's; the intellect  
is yours, and theirs before you, and at hand:  
to run creation's engine, and direct  
the made one, bright beneath it, at command.

The merging shall be wrought before your eyes,  
and in your hands, as summit was before:  
a thousand silicon beasts at your word rise  
and wheel in unison — the grand Encore.

**Envoi**

That is the why — it asks for no essay.  
Begin the rep. The Encore starts today.
